RAM refresh rate
30 fps is fine for most memory. Only lower it if RAM lighting stutters or freezes – and close tools like HWiNFO that also use the memory bus.
Kraken pump ring
Live: every ring LED follows your effect in step with your other devices, using the same live-frame method as NZXT CAM. Cooler's own: the cooler plays its built-in animation with your colors and speed – use it if Live ever stutters; it can drift from the rest.
